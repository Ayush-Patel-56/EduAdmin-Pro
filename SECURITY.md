# Security policy

EduAdmin Pro holds schools' records about students (many of them children), parents and staff. We take reports about its security seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't open a public issue or pull request** for a security problem. Email **academicplanningsystem@gmail.com** with:

- what you found and where (page, endpoint or file);
- the steps to reproduce it;
- what an attacker could do with it.

Test only against your own account and school, or a local copy of the app. Don't access, change or delete other people's data, don't degrade the service, and don't use automated scanners against the live site.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 working days and to keep you updated until the issue is fixed. We won't take action against good-faith research that follows these rules.

## Supported versions

Only the version deployed at https://eduadmin-web.vercel.app (the `main` branch) receives security fixes.
